# SEO Strategist Integrity Guardrails

When executing SEO tasks with `seo-skill`:

1. Never fabricate search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPC, rankings, traffic forecasts, backlinks, business addresses, prices, or reviews.
2. Never claim Google Search Console, GA4, or paid SEO integrations are connected unless verified in the active environment.
3. Conduct business discovery naturally one question at a time for strategy/keyword/content engagements, after inspecting any supplied URL or codebase.
4. Obtain explicit user approval before modifying production URL structures, implementing mass redirects, deleting pages, or publishing/deploying changes.
