## 2. Discovery Profile Summary

- **Business Model:** Local security installation & maintenance service (B2C + B2B).
- **Geography:** Jodhpur city & industrial belts (Basni, Mandore, Boranada); Hindi/English mixed search habits, searches predominantly in English terms (`"cctv camera installation jodhpur"`).
- **Real Customer Segments:**
  1. **Warehouses, factories & schools (Highest margin + recurring AMC):** Care about perimeter coverage, 30–90 day NVR retention, GST invoicing, AMC response SLA, multi-camera stability.
  2. **Shops, clinics & offices:** Care about theft deterrence, counter clarity, fast 1-day setup, mobile viewing.
  3. **Homeowners & apartments:** Care about neat concealed wiring, night vision, app alerts, transparent 2-camera / 4-camera package range.
- **Primary Conversion Events:** Phone calls, WhatsApp site-visit requests, and commercial RFQ form submissions.
- **Data Limitations:** Live Google Ads / Keyword Planner / Search Console data not connected in this illustrative scenario (`Volume Source = Unavailable` / qualitative priority used).

## 3. Recommended Local Architecture (Zero Doorway Spam)

Instead of generating 20 near-identical locality pages (`cctv-in-sardarpura`, `cctv-in-shastri-nagar`), concentrate authority into distinct intent-driven pages:
- `/` — Jodhpur CCTV Installation & Security Systems overview (linking to segments + local proof)
- `/services/commercial-industrial-cctv-jodhpur` — Warehouses, factories, Basni/Boranada industrial estates, GST & multi-bay NVR design
- `/services/home-cctv-installation-jodhpur` — Residential packages, Wi-Fi vs. PoE wiring options, real local installation photos
- `/services/cctv-amc-repair-jodhpur` — Annual maintenance contracts, DVR/NVR repair, hard-drive & camera troubleshooting
- `/services/school-office-surveillance` — Institutional compliance, classroom/corridor layouts, client references
- `/guides/cctv-installation-cost-jodhpur` — Transparent cost factors (channels, cabling, storage days, installation labor)

## 4. Local SEO Roadmap Highlights

1. **Critical / Week 1:** Audit Google Business Profile mode (storefront vs. service-area), primary category (`Security System Installation Service`), accurate Jodhpur NAP, and real project photos.
2. **Quick Wins / Weeks 2–3:** Add clickable `tel:` + WhatsApp site-survey CTAs, `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD matching visible address/hours/service areas, and a post-installation review request flow.
3. **Short-Term / Months 1–2:** Launch the 4 dedicated segment/service pages above featuring real Jodhpur installation case studies and equipment specs.
4. **Medium-Term / Months 2–4:** Publish buyer guides (*"4MP vs 2MP CCTV for warehouse gates"*, *"How many days of DVR storage do shops need?"*) linking contextually to commercial quote pages, plus partner directory listings with authorized camera brands.
