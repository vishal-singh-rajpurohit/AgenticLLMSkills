# Worked Example: B2B SaaS (AI-Powered Email Automation Platform)

*(Illustrative worked scenario demonstrating buyer-committee personas, bottom-of-funnel feature/integration/comparison mapping, and pipeline attribution.)*

---

## 1. Buyer Segments & Search Behavior

1. **Outbound Sales / RevOps Leads (B2B 20–250 employees):**
   - Trigger: Reply rates dropping, domain landing in spam, SDRs spending hours personalizing follow-ups manually.
   - Searches: `"automated cold email personalization software"`, `"hubspot sequence alternative with ai warmup"`, `"email deliverability monitoring tool"`.
   - Trust Needs: CRM bi-directional sync details, domain reputation safeguards, transparent seat/sending pricing, GDPR/SOC2 clarity.
2. **B2B Marketing & Lifecycle Teams:**
   - Trigger: Churn in onboarding flows, poor trial-to-paid activation.
   - Searches: `"behavior triggered onboarding email automation"`, `"customer.io vs <competitor> for saas"`.
3. **Lead-Gen & Marketing Agencies:**
   - Trigger: Managing 15+ client workspaces and inbox warmup cleanly.
   - Searches: `"multi client cold email automation platform"`, `"agency inbox rotation tool"`.

## 3. Anti-Thin-Content Guardrails Applied

- Launch **deep, verified integration pages** only for Tier-1 CRMs/ESP partners where real field mappings, webhook triggers, screenshots, and sync caveats are documented.
- For comparison pages (`"<Brand> vs <Competitor>"`), audit competitor pricing/limits from live documentation with a `"Last verified"` date—never publish unverified claims or auto-swapped comparison templates.
- Measure success via **Organic Trial Signups, Demo Bookings, PQLs, and SQL Pipeline** per landing page in GA4 + CRM, not raw blog pageviews alone.
