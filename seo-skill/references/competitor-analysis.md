# Market & Competitor Analysis

Identify who actually competes for your customer's clicks in search—not just the companies the business owner already knows—and uncover concrete gaps you can win ethically.

## Competitor Taxonomy

Classify competitors across five distinct lenses so blind spots do not skew strategy:

1. **Direct Business Competitors:** Same offerings, same buyer, same market/geography.
2. **Search (SERP) Competitors:** Domains ranking top 10 for your priority commercial/local/product keywords (including marketplaces, directories, or aggregators).
3. **Content Competitors:** Publishers, blogs, or SaaS knowledge bases capturing your audience's informational and comparison searches.
4. **Local Competitors:** Businesses appearing in the Google Local Pack / Bing Places for target locations.
5. **Category Leaders / Benchmarks:** Best-in-class sites setting UX, trust, and topical depth standards even outside your immediate region.

---

## Multi-Axis Competitor Audit Checklist

Inspect live competitor sites (record exact URLs and observation dates) across:

1. **Site Architecture & URL Taxonomy:** How categories, services, industries, locations, and docs/blogs are organized.
2. **Commercial Landing Pages:** Which services/features/categories have dedicated pages vs. lumped onto one generic page.
3. **Content Depth & Originality:** Real examples, pricing clarity, spec tables, installation/case photos, author credentials, original data vs. generic boilerplate.
4. **Search Intent Alignment:** How well top pages satisfy the exact next step the searcher wants.
5. **Internal Linking & Hubs:** Pillar/cluster structure, breadcrumbs, contextual cross-links between educational content and money pages.
6. **Local & Trust Signals (if applicable):** Google Business Profile categories, review count/velocity/content, local proof, certifications, guarantees.
7. **Technical & UX Baseline:** Mobile usability, page speed cues, schema types implemented (do not copy errors!), clear CTAs and frictionless lead/checkout flow.
8. **Backlink & Digital PR Signals (if verified data available):** Types of sites linking to them (associations, news, partners, original tools/studies).
   - *Data integrity note:* If backlink tools (Semrush, Ahrefs, etc.) are unavailable, state clearly that authority/backlink metrics are unmeasured rather than inventing DA/DR scores.

## Evidence & Ethical Guardrails

- **Cite observed URLs and access dates** for every competitor finding in [../templates/competitor-analysis.md](../templates/competitor-analysis.md).
- **Separate observed facts from strategic inferences.** Example: *"Observed: Competitor X has dedicated pages for warehouse, school, and hospital CCTV with project photos (checked 2026-11-25)"* vs. *"Inference: Segment-specific proof likely helps convert institutional buyers."*
- **Never copy, spin, or scrape competitor content.** Beat competitors by bringing real first-hand proof, clearer pricing/specs, stronger local/domain expertise, and better user experience.
- **Avoid fighting unwinnable head terms blindly.** If marketplaces (Amazon, Flipkart, G2, Justdial) hold positions 1–6 for a broad head term, look for high-intent niches, comparison queries, local intent, or specialized sub-categories where specialist authority wins.
