# Ethical Off-Page SEO & Brand Authority

Build durable search authority, referral traffic, and brand trust through **earned editorial coverage, partnerships, and genuine community credibility**—never manipulative link schemes.

## Backlink & Authority Analysis

When backlink tools (GSC Links report, Semrush, Ahrefs, etc.) are connected:
- Evaluate **referring domain quality and topical relevance**, not raw link count alone.
- Distinguish followed editorial links from nofollow/UGC directory profiles, scraper noise, and sitewide template links.
- Check **anchor text distribution** for natural brand/URL/title mix vs. unnatural commercial exact-match over-optimization.
- Audit **linkable assets** on competitor domains: what actually earned their best links? (free calculators, original surveys, open datasets, technical guides, local sponsorships).

**Data Integrity Rule:** If no backlink API or export is available, state explicitly that backlink metrics could not be audited rather than estimating Domain Authority/Rating or link counts.

## Ethical Acquisition Playbooks

### 1. Digital PR & Original Research
- Publish proprietary data, industry benchmarks, pricing surveys, or field testing results that journalists, bloggers, and analysts genuinely cite.
- Offer concise expert commentary from real founders/engineers via journalist inquiry channels when relevant to the business niche.

### 2. Strategic Partnerships & Ecosystem Links
- **Vendors, distributors, and hardware/software partners:** Request listing on authorized installer/partner directories (e.g., Hikvision/CP Plus authorized partner page for a CCTV company; HubSpot/Shopify app marketplace listings for SaaS).
- **Clients & suppliers:** Case studies featured on partner blogs, integration announcements, and genuine testimonials linking back cleanly.
- **Associations & chambers:** Industry bodies, local chambers of commerce, trade organizations, and university/event sponsorships.

### 3. Reputable Directories & Citations
- Submit selectively to authoritative, moderated industry and regional directories where real buyers look (never blast 500 automated low-quality directories).
- Maintain consistent brand entity info (name, domain, description, logo, social handles).

### 4. Unlinked Brand Mentions & Broken Link Reclamation
- Monitor legitimate press/industry mentions of the brand or original research that omit a clickable link and send a polite, helpful outreach note.
- Update external partners pointing to moved or deprecated URLs on your domain (while ensuring proper `301` redirects on your own site).

### 5. Genuine Community Participation & Reviews
- Answer real technical or buying questions helpfully in trade forums, GitHub discussions, Reddit, or industry Slack/Discord communities without dropping spammy links.
- Build a transparent workflow for authentic customer reviews on GBP, Trustpilot, G2, or Capterra depending on business model.

## Strictly Prohibited Practices (Google Spam Policies)

Never recommend or execute:
- Paid link buying/selling designed to pass ranking signals (`dofollow` guest post marketplaces, link farms).
- Private Blog Networks (PBNs) or expired domain link schemes.
- Automated blog comment, forum signature, or profile-link spam.
- Excessive reciprocal link exchanges (`"link to me and I'll link to you"`).
- Fake reviews, fabricated testimonials, or undisclosed sponsored editorial links lacking `rel="sponsored"` / `rel="nofollow"`.
