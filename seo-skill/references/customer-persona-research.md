# Customer & Persona Research

Build search strategy around **real buyers and observable customer behavior**, never fictionalized marketing archetypes.

## Principles

1. **Segment by job-to-be-done and search intent, not arbitrary demographics.** Age or job title only matters when it changes the problem, trust threshold, budget approval, or search vocabulary.
2. **Every distinct customer segment may need a distinct search path.** One generic service page rarely satisfies both an emergency homeowner and a procurement manager buying for 12 warehouses.
3. **Use customer language over internal jargon.** Buyers search the symptoms and outcomes they recognize before they learn vendor terminology.

---

## Multi-Segment Discovery Framework

For each meaningful customer segment discovered in [business-discovery.md](business-discovery.md), capture:

| Dimension | Questions to Answer from Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Segment Definition** | Who are they? (e.g., warehouse operations manager vs. homeowner; RevOps lead at 50–200 person SaaS vs. solo founder) |
| **Trigger Event** | What just happened that made them search *now*? (break-in, compliance audit, moving office, software renewal, broken device) |
| **Core Pain & Desired Outcome** | What frustrates them today, and what does success look like 30–90 days after buying? |
| **Vocabulary & Knowledge Level** | Unaware / Problem-aware / Solution-aware / Product-aware / Most-aware |
| **Decision & Approval Process** | Single buyer (fast, phone/checkout) vs. committee (needs spec sheets, ROI case studies, security/compliance answers) |
| **Objections & Trust Requirements** | Price transparency, warranty, installation turnaround, hidden fees, SOC2/GDPR, local proof, reviews |
| **Alternatives Considered** | DIY, local competitor, marketplace, incumbent SaaS, doing nothing |

### Example Segmentation Contrast (CCTV Installation)

- **Homeowners:** Triggered by travel, package theft, or neighbor incidents; search `"home cctv camera installation near me"`, `"wifi camera vs wired cctv for home"`; care about tidy wiring, mobile app viewing, night clarity, transparent package cost.
- **Commercial / Offices / Retail:** Triggered by shrinkage or staff monitoring; search `"cctv installation for shop"`, `"office surveillance setup cost"`; care about multi-camera storage retention, warranty, quick maintenance SLA.
- **Schools / Institutions / Warehouses:** Triggered by compliance, tender requirements, or large perimeter blind spots; search `"warehouse perimeter cctv system"`, `"school cctv compliance requirements"`, `"amc for cctv cameras"`; care about NVR redundancy, uptime, GST invoicing, AMC contracts, installation references.

## Evidence Sources for Real Customer Language

Collect actual phrasing whenever accessible (and record the source):

1. **First-party sales & support signals:** Call notes, email inquiries, WhatsApp/chat transcripts, RFQ forms, support tickets, onsite installer feedback.
2. **Paid search query reports:** Actual converting search terms from Google Ads (if available).
3. **Live SERP observation:** Autocomplete suggestions, People Also Ask (PAA), related searches, and top-ranking competitor headings/FAQs.
4. **Public customer voice:** Google Business Profile reviews (yours and competitors'), G2/Capterra/Trustpilot reviews (for SaaS), Amazon/Flipkart Q&A and 2–4 star reviews (for e-commerce—revealing real objections!), Reddit, Quora, and niche trade forums.

## Search Journey Mapping

Map how each priority segment progresses across four stages so content and landing pages connect naturally:

1. **Problem / Symptom Stage:** *"why is my cctv footage blurry at night"* / *"low cold email reply rates"* -> Informational guides, diagnostic checklists.
2. **Solution Exploration Stage:** *"wired vs wireless cctv"* / *"ai email automation vs manual sequences"* -> Comparisons, buying guides, cost breakdowns.
3. **Vendor / Product Evaluation Stage:** *"commercial cctv installation jodhpur"* / *"best email automation tool for hubspot"* -> Service/use-case/integration pages, transparent pricing, case studies.
4. **Action / Retention Stage:** Quote, demo, trial, checkout, installation scheduling, post-purchase documentation & AMC/renewal support.
