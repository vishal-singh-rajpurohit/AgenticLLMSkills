# Local SEO Playbook

Activate this workflow whenever a business serves customers at physical locations or within defined geographic service areas (e.g., installers, clinics, retail stores, restaurants, agencies, contractors).

## Core Integrity Guardrails

- **Never fabricate** street addresses, virtual/coworking office storefronts, fake service areas, local phone numbers, or customer reviews.
- **Never generate doorway city/neighborhood pages** (e.g., 40 pages with identical copy where only the city/locality name changes—this violates Google's Spam Policies for doorway/scaled content abuse).
- **Only build a dedicated location page** when you can provide genuinely distinct local value: real projects/photos from that city, local team/branch details, local testimonials, city-specific regulations/conditions, and authentic service logistics.

---

## Local Audit & Strategy Checklist

### 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) & Bing Places
- **Eligibility & Mode:** Storefront (customers visit address) vs. Service-Area Business (SAB—visits customers; hide residential address per GBP rules and declare realistic service areas, typically within ~2 hours' drive).
- **Primary & Secondary Categories:** Select the single most accurate primary category (e.g., `Security System Installation Service`), plus truthful secondary categories covering actual services.
- **NAP Consistency:** Exact match of **Name, Address, Phone** across website header/footer/contact page, GBP, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, and reputable directories. Never keyword-stuff the business name on GBP.
- **Services, Products & Attributes:** Populate real services with clear descriptions, genuine hours, WhatsApp/booking links, and authentic project/team photos (avoid stock photos).
- **Posts & Q&A:** Seed accurate customer FAQs and answer real questions promptly.

### 2. Local Keyword Discovery
Target combinations reflecting how locals actually search:
- `<service> in <city>` / `<service> <city>` / `<service> near me`
- `<service> for <segment> <city>` (e.g., `"warehouse cctv installation jodhpur"`, `"home cctv camera installation jodhpur"`)
- Neighborhood/industrial-zone modifiers only where people actually search them and the business genuinely serves there.
- Problem + local service queries (`"cctv repair service jodhpur"`, `"cctv amc provider"`).

### 3. On-Site Local Architecture
- **One strong City/HQ Hub** when operating out of a single city—do not split one city into 15 thin locality pages. Instead, showcase neighborhoods and industrial estates served naturally inside the main city/service pages with real project examples!
- **Multi-location / multi-city:** Unique URL per real branch or primary service city (`/locations/jodhpur/`, `/locations/udaipur/`) linked cleanly from navigation/footer and cross-linked with relevant service pages.
- **Contact & Footer Signals:** Clickable `tel:` link, clear operating hours, real address (for storefronts) or declared service areas, embedded Google Map on Contact/Location pages, and clear local trust proof (local GST/registration where standard, warranty terms, response SLA).

### 4. `LocalBusiness` Structured Data
Implement JSON-LD using the most specific Schema.org subtype (`LocalBusiness`, `ProfessionalService`, `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`, `MedicalClinic`, etc.) matching **only** visible, truthful page details:
- `name`, `url`, `telephone`, `@id`
- `address` (`PostalAddress`—only if physical storefront/office is publicly shown on the page)
- `areaServed` (for service-area businesses)
- `openingHoursSpecification`, `geo` (only real coordinates), `sameAs` (verified official social/GBP links).

### 5. Local Citations & Genuine Local Links
- Audit and standardize major regional & industry directories (e.g., Google, Bing, Apple Maps, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART for India; Yelp, BBB, Apple Maps for US—focusing on quality/accuracy rather than automated spam blasts).
- Build real local authority: chamber of commerce, local vendor/distributor partner pages, local sponsorship/event coverage, regional press, and housing/industry associations.

### 6. Reviews Strategy (Ethical & Sustainable)
- Establish a consistent post-completion workflow asking happy customers via SMS/WhatsApp/email for an honest Google review (never pay for, gate, or fabricate reviews).
- Encourage customers naturally to mention the service performed and area (without scripting fake reviews).
- Respond professionally to every positive and negative review—demonstrating accountability to prospective buyers reading the profile.
