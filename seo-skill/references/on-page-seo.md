# On-Page SEO & Internal Linking Strategy

Turn search-intent research into clear, scannable, high-converting pages where search engines understand the primary topic and users immediately see they are in the right place.

## On-Page Elements Checklist

### 1. Title Tags & Meta Descriptions
- **Unique per indexable URL.** Never ship one fallback site title/description across dynamic product, category, or blog routes.
- **Title tag:** Put the primary topic/intent clearly near the front, reflect real differentiators or location/year only when truthful, and keep scannable (~50–60 characters display budget, remembering Google rewrites overly stuffed or vague titles).
- **Meta description:** Summarize the concrete value proposition, specs/scope, and next step (~120–155 characters) to earn qualified clicks—not keyword lists.

### 2. Heading Hierarchy & Content Structure
- One clear `<h1>` matching the core page promise (aligned with the title tag, not identical boilerplate).
- Logical nested `<h2>` and `<h3>` sections answering the exact sub-questions buyers in that intent stage ask. Never skip heading levels purely for visual font styling—use CSS classes for styling.
- Lead with direct clarity above the fold: what this is, who it is for, why trust it, and the primary action.

### 3. Semantic HTML & Media Optimization
- Use semantic elements (`<main>`, `<article>`, `<nav>`, `<header>`, `<footer>`, `<section>`) and real `<a href>` elements for navigation.
- Write concise, descriptive `alt` attributes describing what the image shows for accessibility and image search—never stuff keywords into `alt` text.
- Use descriptive filenames (`commercial-nvr-cctv-installation-warehouse.webp`, not `IMG_4829.jpg`).

### 4. URL Structure
- Short, human-readable, lowercase hyphenated slugs reflecting hierarchy without excessive folder nesting (e.g., `/services/commercial-cctv-installation` rather than `/cat1/sub2/page?id=89`).
- Avoid baking brittle years into evergreen landing page URLs (`/best-email-automation-tools`, updating the visible year/content rather than changing the slug every January).

## Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links transfer authority, clarify topical relationships, prevent orphan pages, and guide visitors toward conversion.

### Hub-and-Spoke (Pillar & Cluster) Architecture
1. **Commercial & Pillar Hubs:** Core category, service, or high-level topic pages link down to every relevant supporting guide, sub-service, or product line.
2. **Supporting Spokes:** Informational blog posts, tutorials, and comparison pages link contextually **up** to the canonical commercial/pillar hub (and laterally to closely related spokes).
3. **Avoid Funnel Dead Ends:** Informational guides should naturally reference the relevant service, product, or free tool where it genuinely solves the reader's next step.

### Anchor Text Rules
- Use **descriptive, natural anchor text** telling users and crawlers what the linked page covers (`"commercial CCTV installation services"` instead of `"click here"` or `"read more"`).
- Vary anchors naturally; never repeat identical exact-match commercial anchor strings robotically across sitewide templates.
- Avoid sitewide footer keyword-link blocks targeting money pages—keep footers focused on genuine navigation, trust, legal, and primary categories.

### Link Audit Checks
- **Orphan pages:** Every indexable page should receive at least 1–3 contextual or navigational internal links.
- **Important pages buried deep:** Priority revenue pages should sit within 1–3 clicks of the homepage or main navigation.
- **Broken or redirecting internal links:** Update internal `<a href>` links directly to final `200 OK` canonical URLs rather than forcing crawlers and browsers through 301 hops.

## Conversion-Aligned Page UX

Ranking without conversion wastes effort. On commercial and local pages, verify:
- Clear primary CTA visible without scrolling on mobile and desktop (with low-friction options matching buyer readiness: quote, call, WhatsApp, demo, add to cart).
- Specific proof near the CTA (client logos, real installation photos, transparent turnaround/warranty terms, verified reviews, pricing clarity or "starting at" ranges when honest).
- Zero deceptive popups or intrusive interstitials blocking primary mobile content immediately on entry.
