# Evidence-Based Audience Research

Write for **real buyers making real decisions**—never cardboard marketing personas filled with invented hobbies or demographics.

## Grounded Segmentation Rules

1. **Require evidence for every claim.** Base segment descriptions on the discovery interview, existing website/CRM/sales notes, real product reviews, forum discussions, or live search queries. Mark assumptions explicitly.
2. **Segment by decision context.** Separate segments whenever buying triggers, technical fluency, objections, or success criteria differ:
   - *Example (IT Agency):* Non-technical startup founder needing MVP delivery clarity vs. enterprise CTO evaluating security, architecture, and SLA risk.
   - *Example (CCTV Store):* Homeowner wanting simple app alerts and budget clarity vs. factory procurement buyer needing NVR retention math and AMC terms.

## Audience Signals to Extract

For each active segment, document in [../templates/audience-persona.md](../templates/audience-persona.md):
- **Role / Context & Geography:** Where they operate and whether one person decides or a committee approves.
- **Trigger Event:** What problem pushed them to search today.
- **Exact Questions & Phrasing:** Questions sales/support answer repeatedly; live SERP People Also Ask and community phrasing (Reddit, GitHub Discussions, G2/Capterra reviews, product Q&A).
- **Awareness Level:**
  - *Problem-aware:* Knows the symptom, not the product category.
  - *Solution-aware:* Compares approaches (`custom software vs off-the-shelf`).
  - *Product/Vendor-aware:* Evaluates cost, implementation risk, specs, and alternatives.
- **Objections & Trust Proof Needed:** Hidden costs, migration risk, compliance, warranty, turnaround time, real demos/code/examples.
- **Preferred Format & Tone:** Step-by-step tutorial, honest comparison matrix, cost breakdown, video/diagram, or concise executive guide.
