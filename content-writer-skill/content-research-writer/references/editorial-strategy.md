# Topic Discovery & Editorial Strategy

Generate high-leverage topics grounded in real audience needs and business economics—not arbitrary traffic chasing.

## Topic Discovery Channels

Mine opportunities systematically from:
- Recurring pre-sales, onboarding, and support questions.
- Live search suggestions, People Also Ask, and related searches.
- Competitor content gaps (questions buyers ask that incumbents answer poorly).
- Product, feature, integration, and comparison queries (`X vs Y`, `alternatives`, `cost`, `worth it`).
- Common implementation mistakes and troubleshooting issues.
- Industry changes, regulatory updates, and new software/hardware releases.
- Niche community discussions and 2–4 star product reviews (revealing unmet buyer doubts).

## Content Classification by Purpose

Classify each topic deliberately so format matches intent:
- **Education & Discovery:** Educational articles, industry insights, news & updates.
- **Action & Execution:** How-to guides, technical tutorials, documentation, FAQs.
- **Evaluation & Conversion:** Product reviews, comparisons, buying guides, case studies, service pages, product descriptions, conversion landing pages.

---

## Transparent Content Opportunity Score

Never prioritize solely by estimated search volume. Score each candidate topic transparently across:

| Dimension | What High Priority Looks Like |
|---|---|
| **Audience Relevance** | Solves an urgent pain or recurring question for priority buyers |
| **Business Relevance** | Connects naturally to core, high-margin offerings |
| **Search Intent Clarity** | Clear, actionable intent with a well-defined satisfying page format |
| **Originality Potential** | Business has real data, projects, SME knowledge, or product testing to contribute |
| **Search Demand** | Verified GSC impressions, keyword data, or strong multi-source SERP demand (mark `Unavailable` when unmeasured) |
| **Competitive Feasibility** | Realistic chance to outperform current SERP results without impossible domain gaps |
| **Conversion Potential** | Credible path to leads, demos, product evaluation, or assisted revenue |
| **Research & SME Feasibility** | Facts and proof can be verified accurately without guessing |
| **Timeliness** | Urgent industry shift, seasonal window, or product launch alignment |
