# Competitor Content Analysis

Study competing pages to understand **reader expectations and real content gaps**—never to rewrite or spin existing articles.

## 13-Point Inspection Framework

Review top-ranking and direct-competitor URLs across:

1. **Topic Coverage:** Which core subtopics do all top results treat as baseline?
2. **Content Structure:** How do they sequence headings, summaries, tables, and jump links?
3. **Audience Targeting:** Are they writing for beginners, practitioners, buyers, or enterprise evaluators?
4. **Content Depth:** Do they explain *how* and *why* with real edge cases, or stop at surface-level definitions?
5. **Questions Answered:** Which customer objections, pricing questions, or troubleshooting issues do they resolve?
6. **Missing Information (Gaps):** Where do they skip concrete numbers, prerequisites, failure modes, visual steps, or regional context?
7. **Unique Insights:** Do competitors include real test data, case screenshots, or original commentary—or recycle generic advice?
8. **Supporting Visuals:** Diagrams, comparison charts, annotated UI screenshots, or generic stock fluff?
9. **References & Citations:** Do they cite primary docs/data or make unsupported assertions?
10. **Internal Linking:** How do they connect guides to commercial service/product pages?
11. **External Linking:** Do they cite authoritative sources cleanly?
12. **Freshness:** Are specs, screenshots, laws, or framework versions outdated?
13. **Search Intent Match:** Does the page satisfy the reader's next step without forcing premature sales friction?

---

## Anti-Copying Policy & Differentiation Standard

- **Strictly forbidden:** Paraphrasing a competitor's outline paragraph-by-paragraph with synonyms ("skyscraper spinning").
- **How to outperform ethically:**
  - Bring real operational specifics from the Discovery Profile (actual implementation trade-offs, local/industry context, transparent specs).
  - Answer the missing questions competitors glossed over.
  - Organize complex decisions into scannable comparison tables, prerequisites, and verification steps.
  - Cite current primary documentation directly.
