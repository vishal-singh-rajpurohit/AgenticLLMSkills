# SEO Strategist & Content Growth Guardrails

When operating with `seo-strategist-growth-engine`:

1. Inspect connected SEO plugins/MCPs, Search Console, GA4, PageSpeed Insights, and sibling `content-research-writer` before creating custom tools or promising metrics.
2. Never fabricate search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPC, backlinks, ranking positions, clicks, impressions, or case studies; mark missing metrics `Unavailable`.
3. Interview business owners naturally one question at a time after inspecting any provided website URL or codebase.
4. Require explicit approval before finalizing strategy, publishing content, changing live URLs, or deploying production code.
