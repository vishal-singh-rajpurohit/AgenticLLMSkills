# Authoritative SEO Quality & Compliance Rules

Anchor every strategy, technical audit, and content asset in official **Google Search Central** (`developers.google.com/search/docs`) and **Bing Webmaster Guidelines**.

## Core Compliance Standards

- **Search Essentials & People-First Content:** Ensure technical eligibility (crawlable, indexable, clean status codes), clear UX, original insights, real first-hand experience/expertise (E-E-A-T), and accurate citations.
- **Zero-Tolerance Spam Policy Compliance:**
  - **No keyword stuffing**, hidden text/links, cloaking, or deceptive redirects.
  - **No doorway pages** or **Scaled Content Abuse** (e.g., programmatic city/integration clones swapping a single noun without genuine unique value—Google evaluates intent and user utility regardless of human vs. AI production).
  - **No site reputation abuse** (parasitic third-party pages leveraging host domain authority).
  - **No link spam**, paid link schemes, PBNs, automated comment spam, or fake reviews/author bios.
- **AI Search Visibility:** Per Google's *AI Features and Your Website* documentation, AI Overviews and AI Mode draw from core Search ranking and helpful-content systems using retrieval grounding and query fan-out; no special markup hack replaces fundamentals.

---

## Evidence & Provenance Discipline

- **Confirmed Requirements vs. Recommendations:** Clearly distinguish official search-engine limits/rules (e.g., `robots.txt` blocks crawling not guaranteed deindexing; XML sitemap `50,000` URL / `50 MB` limit; `INP <= 200 ms` good threshold) from strategic agency heuristics and hypotheses.
- **No Guarantees:** Never promise page-1 rankings, indexing dates, traffic targets, or rich-result visibility.
