# On-Page SEO, Internal Linking & Conversion Optimization

Optimize every landing page, category, and article for **clarity, search intent satisfaction, crawlable internal discovery, and conversion readiness**.

## On-Page Elements

- **Title Tag & Meta Description:** Unique per indexable route; front-load the primary topic and summarize concrete value/differentiators. Note that Google generates title links and snippets dynamically and may rewrite them—treat tags as strong signals, never guaranteed SERP copy.
- **URL Slug:** Short, readable, lowercase hyphenated paths aligned with site hierarchy; preserve existing ranking URLs unless a migration with `301` redirects is approved.
- **Heading Hierarchy:** One clear `<h1>` matching the page promise, followed by logical `<h2>`/`<h3>` sections answering buyer sub-questions without skipping levels arbitrarily.
- **Images & Media:** Descriptive `alt` text explaining informative visuals (`alt=""` for purely decorative UI icons), explicit dimensions to prevent CLS, compressed formats (WebP/AVIF).
- **Content Quality & Anti-Duplication:** Zero boilerplate swaps across city/category/integration pages (avoid Scaled Content Abuse). Differentiate with real pricing drivers, specs, edge cases, troubleshooting, or verified local proof.

---

## Internal & External Linking Strategy

- **Inspect real routes first:** Never invent internal URLs.
- **Hub-and-Spoke Topology:** Link category/service hubs down to sub-services or cluster guides, and link supporting articles contextually up to relevant commercial/demo pages using natural descriptive anchor text (avoid `"click here"` or forced exact-match repetition).
- **Orphan & Depth Recovery:** Ensure every priority indexable page is reachable via crawlable `<a href>` links from relevant navigation or contextual pages.
- **External References:** Cite primary official documentation, standards, or research cleanly and verify external URLs resolve.

---

## Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Alignment

- **Above-the-Fold Clarity:** State who the offer is for, what problem it solves, and the next action within seconds.
- **Objection Handling & Proof:** Place verified customer outcomes, real specs/pricing transparency, certifications, or FAQs directly near decision points.
- **Funnel-Matched CTAs:** Match the call-to-action to query intent (e.g., `"Book technical scoping call"` on commercial service pages; `"Compare deployment checklists"` or low-friction tool trial on middle-of-funnel guides).
