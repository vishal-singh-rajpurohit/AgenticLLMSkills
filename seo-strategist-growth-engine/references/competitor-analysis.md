# Market & Competitor Analysis

Identify direct business rivals **and** indirect SERP/content competitors capturing your target audience's searches, backed by verified URLs and observation dates. Keep competitor types distinct: **direct business**, **indirect/substitute**, **search/SERP** (publishers, marketplaces, directories, communities), and **brand benchmark** competitors.

## Competitor Audit Steps

1. **Discover real search competitors:**
   - If Semrush MCP is connected, run `competitors_research` → `domain_organic_organic`, `domain_domains` (keyword gap up to 5 domains), and `organic_research` → `resource_organic_unique` (content gap) following [../integrations/provider-adapters/semrush-mcp.md](../integrations/provider-adapters/semrush-mcp.md).
   - Otherwise, run live SERP checks across core commercial, comparison, local, and problem queries to see who actually owns page-1 real estate—never rely solely on brands named by the owner.
2. **Inspect competing landing & content pages:**
   - **Architecture & URLs:** Dedicated service, use-case, integration, category, or city pages vs. consolidated hubs.
   - **Customer language:** How competitors frame pain points, compliance constraints, and ROI outcomes.
   - **Content structure & depth:** Heading flow, spec tables, pricing transparency, calculators/checklists, FAQs, and internal link hubs.
   - **Conversion & proof signals:** Above-the-fold CTA clarity, trust badges, verifiable case evidence, and friction points.
   - **Underserved intents & gaps:** Questions buyers ask that competitors gloss over (e.g., real pricing drivers, implementation timelines, local regulations, edge cases, honest trade-offs).
3. **Differentiate ethically:**
   - Never copy, paraphrase, or spin competitor text.
   - Outperform incumbents by adding genuine first-hand expertise, original data/screenshots/worked examples, clearer decision matrices, and lower-friction conversion paths.
4. **Cite evidence cleanly:**
   - Log every competitor URL, observed fact, provider snapshot, and access date in [../templates/competitor-analysis.md](../templates/competitor-analysis.md), keeping verified observations strictly separate from strategic inferences.
