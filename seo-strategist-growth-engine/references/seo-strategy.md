# SEO Strategy & Phased Growth Roadmap

Translate verified discovery, technical audits, keyword clusters, and competitor gaps into a 5-phase roadmap built around **qualified pipeline and sustainable authority**.

## Existing vs. New Website Starting Paths

- **Existing Websites:** Start with crawl/indexability triage, GSC quick-win recovery (positions 4–20 + high-impression low-CTR pages), duplicate/cannibalization cleanup, and core commercial page CRO upgrades before launching broad new content.
- **New Websites:** Establish clean URL hierarchy and information architecture first, ship core high-intent money pages (services/product/categories/use-cases) with clean technical foundations, then build focused topical clusters around revenue niches rather than competing blindly on high-KD head terms.

---

## 5-Phase Growth Architecture

1. **Phase 1 — Foundation:** Resolve crawl/indexability blockers, refine URL/site architecture, clean XML sitemaps and canonicals, strengthen core money/landing pages, metadata, CWV health, and accurate Schema.org markup.
2. **Phase 2 — Keyword Strategy:** Finalize intent-mapped keyword clusters, close high-intent competitor gaps, launch missing commercial/use-case/local/category pages, and establish hub-and-spoke internal links.
3. **Phase 3 — Content Strategy:** Ship topical authority pillars, supporting educational clusters, comparison/alternatives pages, verified case studies, and conversion-focused assets matched to buyer objections.
4. **Phase 4 — Ethical Authority Development:** Build original research/benchmarks, free utility tools/calculators, digital PR, industry publications, and genuine partner/vendor citations. **Never** recommend paid spam links, PBNs, link exchanges at scale, or automated comment links.
5. **Phase 5 — Optimization & Growth:** Refresh decaying or underperforming URLs, run CTR and internal-link improvements, tighten landing-page conversion UX, and monitor controlled ranking experiments.

---

## Business-Model Nuances

- **Local / Service Agency:** Dedicated service pages + genuinely unique city/region pages (real local projects/compliance/pricing—never doorway clones) + Google Business Profile consistency.
- **E-Commerce:** Clean category taxonomy, canonical/noindex controls on faceted filter traps (`?color=&sort=`), rich unique product specs, and out-of-stock lifecycle handling (keep seasonal/returning pages live with alternatives instead of abrupt 404s).
- **B2B SaaS:** Problem-aware guides + integration pages + `"X vs Y"` / `"alternatives"` pages + concrete use-case landing pages tied directly to demos/trials.
