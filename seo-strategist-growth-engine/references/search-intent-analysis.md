# Search Intent & Live SERP Analysis

Inspect actual search results for every priority keyword cluster—never guess page format from keyword phrasing alone.

## Live SERP Inspection Signals

Record country, language, and device (`mobile`/`desktop`) while inspecting top-ranking results across:
1. **Dominant Intent:** Informational, Navigational, Commercial Investigation, Transactional, Local, or Mixed.
2. **Ranking Page Types:** Dedicated service pages, SaaS landing pages, product/category listings, comparison guides, tutorials, documentation, tools/calculators, or local map/directory pages.
3. **Observable SERP Features:** Local Pack, Shopping grid, Video carousel, Featured Snippet, People Also Ask, Discussions/Forums, AI Overviews / AI Mode links.
4. **Content Depth & Subtopics:** Required pricing factors, technical specs, integrations, compliance details, comparisons, or local trust signals that top pages consistently address.

---

## Format Selection Matrix

| Observed SERP Pattern | Recommended Format | Anti-Pattern to Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Service providers / Local Map Pack | Dedicated **Service** or **Location Page** | Writing an informational blog post expecting service leads |
| Shopping grids / category grids | **Category** or **Product Page** | Stuffing product terms into a generic essay |
| `"X vs Y"`, `"alternatives"`, `"best..."` | **Comparison Article** or **Buying Guide** | Hiding unbiased trade-offs behind pure ad copy |
| `"how to"`, errors, architecture concepts | **Tutorial**, **Blog Guide**, or **Resource Docs** | Thin sales pitch with zero actionable solution |
| Real client implementations & ROI | **Verified Case Study** | Fabricating numbers, logos, or testimonials |
| Narrow recurring buyer objections | **FAQ Section** on parent landing page | Splitting thin 2-sentence FAQs into orphan URLs |

---

## Handling Mixed Intent & AI Search Features

- **Mixed-intent SERPs:** When top 10 mixes guides and product/service pages, structure a hybrid page (clear solution/commercial framing up front + substantive educational/comparison depth below) or inspect which intent drives qualified pipeline.
- **AI Overviews & AI Mode:** Per Google Search Central (*AI Features and Your Website*), there are no special "GEO/AEO hacks" separate from Search Essentials. Systems use retrieval grounding and query fan-out—earn inclusion with direct, well-structured, factual answers supported by primary sources and clean crawlable HTML.

Do **not** confuse mentioning a keyword with satisfying search intent.
